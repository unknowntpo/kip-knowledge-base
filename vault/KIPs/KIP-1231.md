## Summary

The ConsoleProducer has two options, --batch-size and --max-partition-memory-bytes, both of which configure the underlying batch.size producer config. One of them should be removed.

A previously discarded KIP-717 suggested removing --batch-size. However, this KIP argues for the opposite. Retaining --batch-size is more straightforward and aligns better with the terminology used throughout the Kafka community.
