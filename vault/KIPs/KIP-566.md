## Summary

added the ConsumerInterceptor interface to support the introspection, and potentially, modification of messages before they are consumed. Many uses of message interception such as metrics collection/monitoring (which was a primary motivator for KIP-42) require the interceptor to keep some sort of per-partition state. Unfortunately, the ConsumerInterceptor interface does not provide a way for the interceptor to be made aware of consumer rebalances, making maintenance of such state difficult. To remedy this, we propose having ConsumerInterceptor extend ConsumerRebalanceListener.
