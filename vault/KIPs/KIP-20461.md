## Summary

offsets.retention.minutes controls how long committed consumer group offsets are retained after a group goes empty or stops committing to a topic. The default is 7 days (10080 minutes). There is currently no way to disable this expiry — only finite positive values are accepted.

This creates a reliability gap: if an application crashes and is not noticed for longer than the retention window, the consumer group is deleted. When the application is restarted it must fall back to auto.offset.reset (typically latest or earliest), potentially skipping or reprocessing a large amount of data.

Topic-level retention.ms already supports -1 to mean "retain forever". offsets.retention.minutes should offer the same option for consistency and for operators who want guaranteed offset durability regardless of inactivity duration.
