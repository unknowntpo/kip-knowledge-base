## Summary

At the moment, the default settings of the Kafka Producer provide at-most once delivery without ordering guarantees per partition. This is the weakest delivery guarantee Kafka provides. Since Kafka 0.11.0, Kafka's strongest delivery guarantee is exactly once, in order, delivery per partition.

The next release of Kafka is 1.0.0, and it seems like a good time to have Kafka ship with its strongest delivery guarantees as the default.

Let's clarify the terminology we use in the rest of this document to ensure we are all on the same page.
