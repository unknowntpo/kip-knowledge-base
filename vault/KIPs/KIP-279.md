## Summary

We have observed the edge case in the replication failover logic which can cause divergence between logs. This occurs with clean leader election and in spite of the improved truncation logic from . There are also known unclean leader election edge cases that KIP-101 did not address. This KIP proposes a change to the Kafka Replication Protocol, a follow up to KIP-101, that will fix edge cases that lead to log divergence for both clean and unclean leader election configs.

We first give two examples when logs can diverge.
